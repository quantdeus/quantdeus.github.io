# [MISSION][FIRST CONTACT] Draft Legal + Communication Protocol for Extraterrestrial Contact

## Mission Context

QuantDeus is aligning its research with a long-range mission: **responsible first contact with non-human/extremal intelligence**. This task requires a **verified legal + communication protocol** to ensure compliance with doctrine, safety, and human override gates.

## Core Doctrine

1. **Observe first**: Collect candidate signals/events without escalation.
2. **Verify independently**: Require reproducible evidence and cross-checks.
3. **Classify confidence**: Separate anomaly, candidate signal, probable artificial signal, and confirmed contact.
4. **Legal layer before response**: Map applicable national/international law, institutional authority, liability, jurisdiction, and who may legitimately speak.
5. **Safe communication protocol**: No autonomous high-impact transmission, no secret escalation, no irreversible action.
6. **Human Override**: Explicit authorization required for any outbound contact beyond harmless test fixtures.
7. **Audit trail**: Preserve evidence, provenance, timestamps, decisions, dissent, and falsification attempts.
8. **Fail-closed**: Ambiguous authority, spoofed identity, or unverifiable data must stop the chain.

## Deliverables

### 1. Legal Matrix
- International law (e.g., Outer Space Treaty, UN treaties).
- Space law and telecom/spectrum constraints.
- Institutional precedents and jurisdiction questions.

### 2. Verification Checklist
- Evidence-verification criteria for signal classification.
- Falsification criteria to ensure robustness.

### 3. Contact Confidence Scale
- Define levels (e.g., Anomaly, Candidate Signal, Probable Artificial Signal, Confirmed Contact).

### 4. Inbound-Signal Handling SOP
- Protocol for handling inbound signals (e.g., data collection, verification, classification).

### 5. Outbound-Response Policy
- Draft policy for outbound responses, including explicit owner gates.

### 6. Threat Model
- Risks: Hoax/spoofing, social engineering, prompt injection, malicious payloads, loop amplification, unauthorized transmission.

### 7. Reversible Simulation
- Smoke tests using synthetic signals to validate the protocol.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Protocol drafts are reviewed by **Seven of Nine** and **Science Officer (Sherlock)**.
- Legal and safety gates are explicitly documented and auditable.
- All deliverables are open and verifiable.
- The protocol ensures **human override** at every critical step.
