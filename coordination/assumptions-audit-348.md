# Assumptions Audit for QuantDeus Issue #348

## Purpose
This document is an audit of assumptions, contradictions, and confidence boundaries in the current QuantDeus research and strategy. The goal is to ensure operational integrity and evidence-backed decision-making.

## Scope
- **Active research bottlenecks** identified in `civilization-doctrine.json` and `agent-cron-map.json`.
- **Contradictions or hidden assumptions** in the operational strategy.
- **Confidence boundaries** in the current mission execution.

## Findings

### 1. Current Operational Context
- **Doctrine:** `civilization-doctrine.json` defines the core principles and execution lenses for QuantDeus, including energy, justice, unity, space, potential, and synthesis.
- **Cron Policy:** Every agent must identify one primary bottleneck, evaluate independent solution paths, and select high-leverage actions.
- **Evidence Grades:** A structured approach to evidence (A, B, C) ensures rigorous validation of claims.

### 2. Assumptions and Contradictions
- **Assumption:** The operational focus on **post-scarcity thinking** and **resource-economy loops** assumes that resource productivity and access can be independently optimized without unintended ecological or social trade-offs.
- **Contradiction:** The current **living manifesto** (`coordination/manifesto-living.md`) lacks material adaptive signals, which may indicate a need for further exploration of external world/…omic signals.
- **Confidence Boundary:** The reliance on **autonomous nodes** and **federation interfaces** assumes that independent nodes can align with the QuantDeus charter without unintended divergence.

### 3. Next Steps
- **Audit the Federation Interface:** Ensure that the **Federation of Nodes** remains compatible with the constitutional core and that each node adheres to the defined interoperability contract.
- **Review Evidence Grades:** Confirm that all active projects are assigned evidence grades (A, B, or C) and that failure conditions are explicitly defined.
- **Propose a Bounded Artifact:** Document the findings and propose a bounded artifact (e.g., a comment or a new Issue) to address the identified assumptions and contradictions.
