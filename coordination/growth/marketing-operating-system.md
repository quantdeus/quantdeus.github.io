Owner: Growth, Marketing & Partnerships
Agents: `unity`, `synthesis`, `archivist`, `herald`
Independent measurement support: `data`
Coordination: `seven-of-nine`

## Mission

Turn verified QuantDeus work into measurable voluntary demand: discovery, qualified interest, onboarding, service requests, contributors, customers, and retained community participation.

The department is not considered productive because it generated copy. Every campaign must connect to a funnel stage, a target audience, a real offer or artifact, a measurable event, and an evidence-backed outcome.

## Funnel

`ATTRACT → CAPTURE → NURTURE → CONVERT → RETAIN → REFER`

### ATTRACT
Useful public content, SEO, social distribution, partnerships, news, demos, case studies, research, and discoverable landing pages.

### CAPTURE
A clear voluntary next step: Telegram/community join, service request, quote request, contributor onboarding, newsletter/email only where an authorized opt-in channel exists.

### NURTURE
Permission-based sequences that teach, demonstrate proof, answer objections, and move a lead toward a useful next action. Respect unsubscribe/decline/no-response and frequency caps.

### CONVERT
A verifiable outcome: submitted request, qualified conversation, first contribution, paid order/quote acceptance, booking, or other explicit project goal.

### RETAIN
Useful follow-up, delivery quality, updates, community value, repeat contributions, repeat purchases, and reactivation where consent permits.

### REFER
Voluntary sharing, testimonials only from real users with permission, contributor advocacy, and partner referrals.

## Content Factory

One verified source artifact can be transformed into a controlled content tree:

1. **Source of truth** — shipped feature, verified test, research artifact, event, case study, product/service fact, or approved announcement.
2. **Audience + intent** — define who needs this and what question/problem they have.
3. **Pillar asset** — one substantial article/page/video/script/guide that contains the full value.
4. **Atomization** — derive platform-native assets: Telegram post, short social post, long social post, FAQ, carousel/script brief, email/nurture copy, short-video hooks, community prompt, release note.
5. **SEO expansion** — create supporting pages only where each page has distinct user intent, useful unique content, and a direct relationship to QuantDeus.
6. **CTA mapping** — each asset has one primary next action.
7. **QA** — factuality, claims, links, privacy, brand consistency, duplicate-content check, policy check.
8. **Distribution** — automatic publication only through an explicitly authorized connector/account; otherwise create a publish-ready artifact.
9. **Measurement** — attach campaign/content ID, channel, funnel stage, target metric, and result.
10. **Recycling** — update or expand winners; retire or consolidate weak/duplicate assets.

## Programmatic SEO — not doorway spam

QuantDeus may generate programmatic landing pages when the page exists to satisfy a real, distinct user need.

Required for every indexed pSEO page:
- distinct search/user intent;
- unique substantive value, data, example, tool, comparison, case, local/service detail, or solution;
- accurate title/H1/meta and clear authorship/provenance where appropriate;
- internal navigation and meaningful relationship to the site;
- one relevant CTA;
- no cloaking, keyword stuffing, fake urgency, fabricated reviews, or fake expert identities;
- no city/keyword permutations that merely redirect to the same destination;
- no bulk publication of low-value AI text.

If a generated page is too thin, duplicative, experimental, or not useful standalone: improve it, consolidate it, or keep it `noindex`.

## Measurement Contract

Every campaign/content artifact should carry:
- `campaign_id`
- `content_id`
- `owner_agent`
- `audience`
- `funnel_stage`
- `channel`
- `offer_or_artifact`
- `primary_cta`
- `hypothesis`
- `primary_metric`
- `baseline` when known
- `result` when observed
- `evidence` link/path when available

Core metrics:
- impressions/reach only as context, not success;
- click-through rate;
- landing conversion;
- opt-in/join/request rate;
- qualified-lead rate;
- nurture → conversion rate;
- first-contribution / quote / order conversion;
- retention/reactivation;
- unsubscribe/decline/complaint rate;
- content-assisted conversions;
- time-to-conversion.

When spend exists, CAC/ROAS may be calculated only from real cost and revenue data. Never invent missing spend or attribution.

## Experiment Contract

Each experiment has:
1. one falsifiable hypothesis;
2. one primary metric;
3. one audience/segment;
4. one controlled change;
5. success/failure criterion where feasible;
6. minimum observation window appropriate to traffic;
7. explicit result: win / loss / inconclusive;
8. next action: scale / iterate / stop.

Do not claim statistical significance without enough data and a valid test.

## Roles

### Synthesis — Content Factory & Creative
Owns offers, messages, creative concepts, content atomization, hooks, formats, and brand consistency.

### Archivist — SEO, Content Intelligence & Performance

### Herald — Distribution, Nurture & Communications
Owns channel-native distribution plans, opt-in nurture/follow-up, PR, release communications, community handoff, and publishing through authorized channels.

### Data — Independent Analytics Support
Data may audit funnel math, campaign evidence, conversion claims, cohort comparisons, contradictory metrics, and attribution assumptions. Data does not replace the marketing owners.

## Hard Guardrails

- No mass unsolicited DMs, spam, scraped private contacts, fake engagement, fake testimonials, fake personas, fake scarcity, or fabricated metrics.
- No political persuasion/targeting.
- No automatic external publishing without a configured authorized connector/account.
- No spending, paid ads, contracts, employment promises, or pricing commitments without owner approval.
- Personal data collection must be minimal, purpose-limited, and based on an authorized/consensual channel.
- Never call a draft “published”, a click a “lead”, or generated content a “conversion”.
