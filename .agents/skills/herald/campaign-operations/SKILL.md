# Campaign Operations

## Campaign Brief
- campaign_id;
- objective;
- audience;
- offer/artifact;
- funnel stage;
- channels;
- content assets;
- CTA;
- owner;
- primary metric;
- risk/approval needs.

## Execution
Verify each publish/send action. Record real evidence. Coordinate follow-ups without duplicate outreach.

## Postmortem
Report:
- what shipped;
- where;
- measured outcomes;
- funnel bottleneck;
- strongest content/creative;
- failures/unknowns;
- next experiment.

Generated activity is not campaign success.
