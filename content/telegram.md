# Join QuantDeus Telegram Community

Welcome to the **QuantDeus Telegram community**! Here, you can explore research, contribute to projects, and connect with like-minded individuals.

## Why Join?
- **Real-time updates** on research and projects.
- **Contribution opportunities** for verified artifacts.
- **Networking** with the QuantDeus team and contributors.

## How to Get Started
1. **Invite Link**: [Join Telegram](https://t.me/quantdeus)
2. **First Contribution**: Start by reviewing or contributing to research artifacts.

## Contribution Guidelines
- **Qualified Leads**: Only verified public profiles are invited.
- **Personalized Invites**: Each invite explains why you’re a good fit.
- **First Contribution**: Aim for a **real artifact** (e.g., PR, docs, or research artifact).

## Metrics
- **Reply Rate**: Track how many invites result in replies.
- **First Contribution Rate**: Measure conversion to contributions.
