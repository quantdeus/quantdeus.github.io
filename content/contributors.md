# Contributor Onboarding

Welcome to the **QuantDeus Contributor Onboarding** guide! This page helps you get started with your first contribution.

## Why Contribute?
- **Impact**: Your work directly shapes QuantDeus research, projects, and community.
- **Growth**: Join a global network of contributors.
- **Recognition**: Contributions are publicly acknowledged.

## How to Contribute
1. **Qualified Leads**: Only verified public profiles are invited.
2. **First Contribution**: Start with a **small, impactful task** (e.g., docs, research artifact, or PR).

## Next Steps
- **Explore Open Issues**: [View Contributor Tasks](https://github.com/quantdeus/quantdeus.github.io/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Aagent%3Aunity)
- **Join Telegram**: [Join Community](https://t.me/quantdeus)

## Metrics
- **Reply Rate**: Track how many invites result in replies.
- **First Contribution Rate**: Measure conversion to contributions.
