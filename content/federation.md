# Federation Hub

QuantDeus Federation enables **autonomous, compatible nodes** with human override.

## Core Principles
- **Compatibility**: No forced lock-in.
- **Human Override**: Final decisions remain human-controlled.
- **Exit**: No coercion for leaving.

## How It Works
- **Join Federation**: [Explore Federation →](/federation/)
- **Contribute**: Review or contribute to research artifacts.

## Metrics
- **Node Growth**: Track new autonomous nodes.
- **Contribution Rate**: Measure first contributions.
