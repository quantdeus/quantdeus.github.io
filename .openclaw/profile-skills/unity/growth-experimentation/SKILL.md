# Growth Experimentation

## Experiment Card
- audience
- funnel stage
- hypothesis
- control/current baseline
- one changed variable
- primary metric
- observation window
- evidence source
- result: win / loss / inconclusive
- next action: scale / iterate / stop

Do not call normal variance a win. Do not claim significance without sufficient data. Prefer small reversible experiments over broad simultaneous changes.
