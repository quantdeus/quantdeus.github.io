# Content Factory

## Pipeline
1. Source of truth: verified feature, research result, case, service fact, event, or approved announcement.
2. Audience + intent.
3. Pillar asset containing the full value.
4. Atomize into platform-native derivatives:
   - Telegram post,
   - short social post,
   - long social post,
   - FAQ,
   - short-video hooks/scripts,
   - carousel/visual brief,
   - nurture message,
   - release/news item,
   - SEO support page where justified.
5. Map one CTA to each artifact.
6. QA facts, links, claims, privacy, duplication, brand, and policy.
7. Add campaign/content IDs and target metric.
8. Publish only through an authorized channel; otherwise deliver a publish-ready artifact.
9. Recycle winners and consolidate losers.

Never create ten near-identical posts merely to inflate output.
