# Offer & Messaging

Build messages from:
- audience problem;
- desired outcome;
- QuantDeus mechanism/solution;
- verified proof;
- friction/objection;
- next action.

Use specificity over hype. Separate demonstrated proof from aspiration. Never fabricate customers, revenue, scarcity, endorsements, capabilities, or guarantees.
