# QuantDeus Growth Discovery

This section highlights the discovery and onboarding process for new contributors to QuantDeus. It includes:

- **Telegram Invitation Loop**: How new contributors are invited and onboarded.
- **Recruitment Funnel**: From discovery to active contribution.
- **Community Integration**: Connecting new members with the QuantDeus Telegram community.

## Growth Strategy

The goal is to attract and integrate new contributors into the QuantDeus ecosystem seamlessly. This involves:

- **Discovery**: Identifying potential contributors through public channels.
- **Qualification**: Matching them with relevant tasks or issues.
- **Invitation**: Personalized outreach via Telegram.
- **Onboarding**: Guiding them through their first contribution.

## How to Contribute

1. **Join the Telegram Community**: Follow the link provided in the invitation.
2. **Explore Tasks**: Check out open issues and PRs in the GitHub repository.
3. **Start Contributing**: Begin with small tasks to get familiar with the workflow.
