# Contribute to QuantDeus

## Mission
QuantDeus is a community of human contributors, researchers, and builders working toward a shared future. We welcome everyone who shares our values: **human dignity, voluntary participation, and measurable progress**.

## How to Contribute

### 1. Qualify for a Task

- Browse the **[active contributor growth sprint](https://github.com/quantdeus/quantdeus.github.io/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3Agrowth%3Arecruitment+label%3Acoord%3Aready)** for open tasks.
- Select a task that aligns with your skills and interests.

### 2. Invitation & Onboarding

- Unity Agent qualifies candidates against the selected task.
- Synthesis Agent packages a personalized invitation.
- Archivist maintains discoverability and contribution entry points.

### 3. First Contribution

- Once invited, follow the instructions to start contributing.
- Your first contribution will be reviewed for quality.

## Contributor Registry

All contributors are registered in the **[contributors.json](https://github.com/quantdeus/quantdeus.github.io/blob/main/coordination/contributors.json)** file. Access is read-only.

## Growth Process

- **No fake contributors or meaningless commits.**
- **Sensitive external outreach requires explicit human approval.**
- **Personalized outreach is limited to public/opt-in collaboration paths.**

## Next Steps

- Visit the **[active growth sprint issue](https://github.com/quantdeus/quantdeus.github.io/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3Agrowth%3Arecruitment+label%3Acoord%3Aready)** to find your next opportunity.
